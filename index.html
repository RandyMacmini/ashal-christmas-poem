🎄 A Christmas Message 🎄

Missing You While on Vacation

Though miles apart this Christmas day,

While I'm on vacation far away,

My thoughts return to you, my dear,

And wish that we could both be here.

The snow falls soft on distant ground,

While Christmas lights dance all around,

But nothing shines quite like the glow,

Of memories from long ago.

The carolers sing their songs so sweet,

Of joy and peace and friends who meet,

Yet in my heart, there's just one voice,

That makes my lonely soul rejoice.

So though I'm here and you're away,

I send my love this Christmas day,

Across the miles, through snow and cheer,

Until we meet again, my dear.

— With love this Christmas season ❤️

Merry Christmas! 🎅 🎁 ⛄
